## 1. Problem Statement

Imagine nine strangers sitting in a hostel common room. Everyone wants to meet new people, but nobody wants to initiate the first conversation.

Most people end up scrolling through their phones instead of interacting with the people around them.

The problem isn't that people don't want to socialize. It's that they don't know how to start.

**Our goal is to make that first interaction natural, comfortable, and fun.**

## 2. Product Overview

Find Your Tribe is a mobile-friendly social experience that helps strangers connect through shared interests and fun group challenges.

People sitting in the same physical location scan a QR code, answer five interesting questions about themselves, and discover a small group of people with similar personalities or interests.

Each group receives a fun challenge that encourages members to interact, laugh, collaborate, and get to know one another.

The experience ends with a team summary and a conversation starter, encouraging participants to continue interacting even after the game finishes.

**The phone brings people together. The real experience happens offline.**

## 3. Core Product Experience

### Step 1: Create a Room

One person acts as the host.

- The host creates a room.
- A unique QR code is generated.
- The QR code represents that particular room.
- Everyone physically present can scan the QR code to participate.
- The host can see how many people have joined.

### Step 2: Scan and Join

Participants scan the QR code using their phones.

- The website opens immediately.
- No app installation is required.
- No login or signup is required.
- Participants enter a nickname.
- Participants select a fun avatar.
- They can immediately begin answering questions.

The joining experience should take only a few seconds.

### Step 3: Answer Five Fun Questions

Every participant answers five short multiple-choice questions.

These questions help us understand their travel preferences, social personality, and interests.

**Question 1: Your dream trip starts tomorrow. Where are you going?**

- 🏖️ Beach escape
- 🏔️ Mountain adventure
- 🏙️ City exploration
- 🌲 Into the wild

**Question 2: What's your travel personality?**

- 🗺️ The planner
- 🎒 Go with the flow
- 🎉 The social butterfly
- 🔍 The curious explorer

**Question 3: You have one free evening. What sounds best?**

- 🎲 Playing games
- 🍕 Exploring food
- 🌆 Exploring the city
- ☕ Deep conversations

**Question 4: What kind of experience excites you most?**

- 😂 Something funny and unexpected
- ⚡ An adventurous experience
- ❤️ Meeting interesting people
- 🌄 Discovering beautiful places

**Question 5: What makes a trip unforgettable?**

- 👥 The people
- 🏞️ The places
- 🍜 The food
- 🎭 The experiences

Participants should complete all five questions in approximately 30–60 seconds.

## 4. Waiting Room

After answering the questions, participants enter a waiting room.

The waiting room displays:

- Number of people who have joined.
- Number of people who have completed their questions.
- A message indicating that the experience will begin shortly.

Example:

"8 explorers have joined! Waiting for everyone to finish."

Once enough participants are ready, the host starts the experience.

## 5. Find Your Tribe — Group Formation

After everyone completes their questions, the application automatically creates groups based on participants' answers.

### Grouping Concept

Participants with similar preferences should ideally belong to the same group.

For example:

**Team 1 — 🏖️ Sunset Squad**
- People who enjoy beaches, relaxing, and spontaneous travel.

**Team 2 — 🏔️ Mountain Explorers**
- People who enjoy adventure, trekking, and exploring nature.

**Team 3 — 🍕 Food Hunters**
- People who enjoy food, city exploration, and social activities.

These are examples. Actual groups and names should reflect participants' answers.

### Grouping Rules

- Groups should contain approximately 3–4 people where possible.
- People with similar answers should be grouped together.
- Every participant must be included.
- Nobody should be left alone because their interests are different.
- Groups should remain reasonably balanced.
- If an exact match is unavailable, the participant should be placed in the most suitable available group.
- The experience should work with both small and large groups.

For example:

| Participants | Groups |
|---|---|
| 2–3 | One group |
| 4–5 | One group or two suitable smaller groups |
| 6 | Two groups of 3 |
| 7 | Groups of 3 and 4 |
| 8 | Two groups of 4 |
| 9 | Three groups of 3 |
| 10 | Groups of 3, 3, and 4 |
| 12 | Three groups of 4 |

The objective is to create comfortable social groups, not to perform perfect personality matching.

## 6. Tribe Reveal

This is one of the most exciting moments of the experience.

After teams are formed, each participant sees:

- Their team name.
- Their team color.
- Their teammates' nicknames and avatars.
- A fun description of what the team members have in common.

Example:

**🏖️ Welcome to the Sunset Squad!**

Your teammates:
- Alex
- Riya
- Dev

"You all enjoy spontaneous adventures and discovering new places!"

**Your first mission: Find your teammates in the room and say hello!**

Participants then physically find their teammates and gather together.

This is the moment when the product converts digital interaction into a real-world conversation.

## 7. Fun Team Challenges

Once teammates find each other, they receive a fun challenge.

The challenge should encourage everyone to participate.

It should not be something a participant can complete alone.

Challenges should take approximately 2–3 minutes.

### Challenge Ideas

**Challenge 1: The ₹5,000 Adventure**

Your team has ₹5,000 and 48 hours to plan an unforgettable trip.

Every teammate must contribute one idea.

**Challenge 2: Two Truths and One Lie**

Each teammate shares two true statements and one lie about themselves.

The others must guess which statement is false.

**Challenge 3: Find Three Things in Common**

Find three surprising things that all your teammates have in common.

You cannot use the answers from the personality quiz.

**Challenge 4: The Travel Disaster**

One teammate acts out a funny travel disaster without speaking.

The remaining teammates must guess what happened.

**Challenge 5: Mystery Destination**

Describe a travel destination without saying its name.

Your teammates must guess the destination.

**Challenge 6: Build Your Dream Hostel**

Imagine your team owns a hostel.

Each teammate must suggest one crazy feature that would make it unforgettable.

**Challenge 7: Story Chain**

One person starts a travel story.

Every teammate adds a sentence and an unexpected twist.

Together, create the funniest story possible.

**Challenge 8: The Food Challenge**

Everyone shares the strangest or most memorable food they have ever eaten.

The team chooses the most interesting story.

**Challenge 9: Desert Island**

Your team is stranded on an island.

Together, agree on three objects you would want to have and explain why.

**Challenge 10: Plan Tomorrow Together**

Imagine your team has an entire day to explore a new city.

Create a fun itinerary that includes everyone's preferences.

### Challenge Experience

- Each group receives one challenge.
- The challenge can be selected based on the group's interests.
- Everyone in the team must participate.
- Participants discuss and complete the activity in person.
- When finished, they can mark their challenge as completed.

There is no requirement for points, complex scoring, or leaderboards.

The objective is conversation, not competition.

## 8. Final Tribe Reveal

After completing the challenge, participants see a final summary.

Example:

**🎉 You Found Your Tribe!**

**Team:** Sunset Squad

**Your Teammates:**
- Alex
- Riya
- Dev

**What Connects You:**

"You all love spontaneous adventures, discovering new experiences, and meeting interesting people."

**Keep the Conversation Going:**

"If you could plan a trip together tomorrow, where would you go?"

The aim is to encourage participants to continue talking even after leaving the website.

## 9. Feedback

At the end, participants answer two short questions.

**Question 1: Did this experience help you talk to someone new?**

- Yes
- No

**Question 2: Would you enjoy another activity with your teammates?**

- Yes
- Maybe
- No

After submitting feedback, display:

"🎉 Your tribe is waiting! Put your phone away and keep the conversation going."

## 10. Host Experience

The host should have a simple experience.

The host can:

1. Create a room.
2. Share its QR code.
3. See how many participants have joined.
4. See how many participants have completed the questions.
5. Start the group formation process.
6. View the teams that were created.
7. See how many participants completed the experience.

The host should not need to manage teams manually.

## 11. User Experience Requirements

The product must be:

- Mobile-first.
- Extremely easy to understand.
- Fast to open.
- Fun and visually engaging.
- Accessible without signup.
- Accessible without downloading an app.
- Comfortable for introverts and extroverts.
- Suitable for participants who have never met.
- Designed to minimize time spent on phones.

The entire experience should ideally take **5–8 minutes**.

### Design Personality

The visual experience should feel youthful, welcoming, playful, and travel-inspired.

It should have:

- Fun emojis and avatars.
- Engaging question screens.
- Interesting team names.
- Colorful team identities.
- Exciting reveal moments.
- Simple, readable challenges.
- Smooth and lightweight transitions.

The product should feel like a social experience, not a formal questionnaire.

## 12. Measuring Real Usage

Since this is being developed for the goSTOPS Build Sprint, we need to demonstrate how many real people participated.

The product should record:

- Total rooms created.
- Estimated unique participants.
- Number of people who completed the questions.
- Number of teams formed.
- Number of participants who started challenges.
- Number of reported challenge completions.
- Number of participants who submitted feedback.
- Number of participants who reported speaking to someone new.

A simple results dashboard should display these numbers.

Only actual usage data should be presented as results.

## 13. Initial Real-World Validation

Our initial goal is to get approximately **30 real participants**.

We plan to test the application with 3–4 small groups of people.

Possible locations:

- College hostels.
- PG common rooms.
- College canteens.
- Small social gatherings.
- Cafés.

Each group will scan the QR code and experience the full journey.

We will observe whether:

- Participants understand the experience.
- People complete the questionnaire.
- Participants successfully find their teammates.
- Team members actually initiate conversations.
- The challenge is enjoyable.
- Participants continue talking after completing the game.

The goal is to prove that Find Your Tribe can encourage real-world connections.

## 14. What We Are Not Building

To keep the product simple, we will not include:

- User accounts or login.
- Social media profiles.
- Friend requests.
- In-app messaging.
- Voice or video calls.
- AI chatbots.
- Complex gamification.
- Leaderboards.
- Multiple rounds of games.
- Mandatory profile photos.
- Payment features.

These features are unnecessary for solving the immediate problem.

## 15. Success Criteria

Find Your Tribe will be considered successful if:

1. A host can create a room and share its QR code.
2. Participants can join without signup or installation.
3. Participants can easily complete the five questions.
4. Participants are grouped by shared interests.
5. Everyone is included in a team.
6. Participants can identify and physically meet teammates.
7. Teams can complete a fun challenge together.
8. The experience encourages real face-to-face conversations.
9. Participants can submit feedback.
10. We can demonstrate genuine usage and meaningful engagement.

## 17. Final Product Vision

**Find Your Tribe transforms a room full of strangers into small groups of people who have something in common.**

Instead of asking people to approach strangers randomly, it gives them a shared identity, a reason to meet, and something fun to do together.

The journey is intentionally simple:

**Scan QR → Join Room → Answer 5 Questions → Find Your Tribe → Meet Your Teammates → Complete a Challenge → Keep Talking**

### The Core Principle

**We are not building another app that keeps people looking at their phones. We are building an experience that gives them a reason to put their phones down and talk to each other.**
